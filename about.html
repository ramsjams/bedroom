BEDROOM PRODS (Bedroom Productions) is an independent Tunisian music label and creative collective founded in 2020 by artist and producer RamsJamsss.

Operating at the forefront of the North African underground scene, the collective serves as a collaborative hub for alternative youth culture, bridging innovative audio production with visual arts.

Known for its genre-bending catalog, BEDROOM PRODS champions a distinct fusion of alternative rap, experimental electronic music, indie r&b, and ambient soundscapes, driving the new wave of the Tunisian independent music movement.
